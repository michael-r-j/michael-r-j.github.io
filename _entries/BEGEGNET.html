A steel plate repeatedly encounters wet cotton fabric. These encounters leave traces on the fabric, while the plate is steadily changed. Memories bring about a self, that is in turn formed by its stories: the subject and the lost object. It is the process of being as the mediation between affect and effect. {% include exhibited.html %}
