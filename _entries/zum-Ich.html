--- title: 'zum Ich' page: portfolio type: art anchor: zum-Ich startyear: 2026 endyear: 2026 endmonth: 9 range: false loc: exhibitlist: - Lebende-Maschinen imgdir: '/art/work/zum-Ich' published: true --- copper, ink, patina, three copper nails [20 x 30 cm]

Pixels were etched into a copper plate so that the digital could find an analog body, rife in approximation and marks of imperfect translation. A lost object reoccurs in part: its weight can be held and its surface touched. It is all for the flicker of yearning, as heat is taken up only to be dissipated again, while fingerprints leave their traces. In its reflection, I just see my self.

This work is the plate counterpart to the prints of »am Du«.
