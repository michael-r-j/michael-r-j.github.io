An action that actively stained a tablecloth with rust with repeated emptying of a glass of liquid onto a steel table underneath. The performative act of remembering as a redoing of marks of the absent.
Part of the TransArts contribution to the Tolia Curriculum curated by Magdalena Stöger & Leon Hösl.
